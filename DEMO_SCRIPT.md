# AapdaSetu 90-Second Hackathon Demo Script

**Speaker**: "Welcome to AapdaSetu, the AI-powered Disaster Lifecycle Management Platform. Today, we're showing how our system moves from Prediction, to Preparedness, to transparent Response."

## 0:00 - 0:15 | Introduction & Command Hub
*(Show the National Command Hub — Home Overview)*
**Action**: Scroll through the new unified dashboard.
**Speaker**: "This is the National Command Hub. Here, government agencies have a unified view of live incidents, connected RWA societies, and high-risk districts. Note the real-time activity metrics and blockchain ledger feed."

## 0:15 - 0:35 | Phase 3: Heatwave Prediction
*(Click 'Heatwave Forecast' in the sidebar)*
**Action**: Show the interactive India Heat Risk Map. Click on New Delhi.
**Speaker**: "Our multi-agent system constantly monitors environmental data via Open-Meteo and NOAA models. Here, the Heatwave Agent flags New Delhi at EXTREME risk with a 92% probability. It automatically generates tailored recommendations for the government and the public."

## 0:35 - 0:55 | Phase 1: RWA Preparedness
*(Click 'RWA Societies' in the sidebar)*
**Action**: Show the RWA Dashboard, Evacuation Priority Queue, and Resource Inventory.
**Speaker**: "Before a disaster strikes, preparedness is key. Our RWA module connects residential societies directly to the command center. Based on the heatwave threat, our Resource Agent assesses inventory — highlighting a critical shortage of life jackets or medical kits — and prioritizes evacuations."

## 0:55 - 1:15 | Cross-Module Orchestration
*(Go back to Home/Overview. Click 'Simulate Disaster' button)*
**Action**: Click the 'Simulate Disaster' button (Shows the alert popup). 
**Speaker**: "When a threat escalates, our LangGraph-powered Coordinator Agent orchestrates the response. It triggers alerts cascading from the Heatwave module directly to the RWA societies, mobilizing volunteers instantly."

## 1:15 - 1:30 | Phase 2: Blockchain Transparency (Wrap up)
*(Click 'Blockchain Transparency' in the sidebar)*
**Action**: Show the verified records on the blockchain page (or mention it if stubbed).
**Speaker**: "Finally, to guarantee accountability, all verified disaster reports, funding requests, and supply chain movements are permanently logged on the Shardeum blockchain. AapdaSetu ensures every rupee and resource is tracked transparently. Thank you!"
